What we collect

The calculators run entirely in your browser. The sizes, prices and customer names you type are not sent to our server and are not stored by us. Your language choice is saved in your own browser so the site opens in the same language next time.

Advertising and cookies

This site may show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads and measure them. You can manage ad personalisation in your Google account settings.

Accuracy of results

Results are estimates for planning and quoting. Weights use standard material densities and ignore pipe corner radius. Check every measurement and price yourself before cutting, ordering or quoting. The quotation maker produces an estimate, not a tax invoice. Add GST only if you are GST registered, and issue a proper tax invoice from your own billing records. FabricatorTools is not responsible for losses that come from relying on a result.
